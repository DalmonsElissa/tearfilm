Cited designs implemented from their publications
  The divided space-time attention block follows Bertasius, Wang and Torresani,
  "Is Space-Time Attention All You Need for Video Understanding?", ICML 2021.
  The factorised (2+1)D convolution follows Tran et al., "A Closer Look at
  Spatiotemporal Convolutions for Action Recognition", CVPR 2018.
  The frame-pair dual-task design follows El Barche et al., Scientific Reports
  14 (2024).
  The reader-study variance follows the Obuchowski-Rockette decomposition as
  summarised by Obuchowski et al., Academic Radiology 11, 980-995 (2004).
  These are implemented from their published descriptions; no third-party code
  is copied.

Manuscript arithmetic
  The values transcribed in tearfilm_persistence.verification.manuscript are
  read from the article's own tables and are used only to test the internal
  consistency of those tables. They are not measurements of this release.
